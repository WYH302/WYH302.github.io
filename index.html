Yonghao Wu (Leon)

I study multimodal AI and computational imaging: how machines interpret images and sensor measurements, and use them to carry out tasks.

My work includes image reconstruction, visual generation, and agents that plan and use tools. This site brings together my research and projects, alongside essays on language, technology, and society.

Research interests

What I'm working on

Agents and VLA

Tool-using multimodal agents

I study how multimodal agents break down tasks, choose tools, and recognize when they need to stop and reassess.

Generation

Controllable visual generation

From sketch-to-3D models to diffusion and flow-matching reconstruction, I work on making generated images and shapes more controllable and structurally consistent.

Imaging

Computational and optical imaging

I study how single-pixel imaging, compressive imaging, and object-of-interest cameras can recover images from limited measurements while preserving details needed for detection.

Evaluation

Reliable model evaluation

Did the method improve, or did the model accidentally see test information? I work on data splits, training statistics, and the information available at inference time.
