Research map

Four connected lines of work

Agents and VLA

Tool-using multimodal agents

Planning, tool use, task-organization matching, and Vision-Language-Action systems for workflows that expose state, uncertainty, and failure modes.

Generation

Controllable visual generation

Part-aware sketch-to-3D generation, stable prompt adaptation, diffusion and flow-matching reconstruction, and structure-aware latent optimization.

Imaging

Computational and optical imaging

Single-pixel imaging, compressive imaging, object-of-interest cameras, and physically constrained reconstruction for sensing and inspection.

Evaluation

Leakage-controlled research protocols
