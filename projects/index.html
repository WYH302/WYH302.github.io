Selected projects

Research systems for sensing, imaging, and visual intelligence

Selected engineering work across urban infrastructure perception, industrial vision-language compilation, agentic AI workflows, and ocean-sensing systems.

Guangzhou Transportation Bureau Commissioned Project | Core Technical Lead | 2024-2025

Urban Infrastructure Hazard Perception, GPR Defect Analysis, and Bridge Anti-Collision Multi-Modal Visualization Platform

Served as core technical lead delivering an end-to-end infrastructure perception pipeline covering road-hazard detection, GPR-based crack and pothole recognition, sample curation, detection-model training, and object-imaging preprocessing. Delivered a unified multi-modal visualization interface integrating live video, radar signals, automated alarms, and model inference views; the work led to three related invention-patent outputs.

Self-Initiated Research and Engineering Project

StoneVL-Nest / CF-SlabNest: Vision-Language-Driven Verifiable Industrial Nesting Compilation System

Architected a multimodal compilation pipeline translating Chinese natural-language cutting orders and visual defect constraints into geometrically verified stone-cutting nesting layouts. Formalized the core task as a maximum-count irregular nesting problem and designed independent geometric feasibility verification with auditable certificate output.

Self-Initiated Research and Engineering Project

Multi-Agent Social Simulation and LLM-Orchestrated Agentic Workflow Prototype System

Constructed a full-stack multi-agent social simulation with React, Phaser, and Node.js, supporting persistent character memory, structured dialogue scheduling, and longitudinal relationship evolution. Co-designed a deterministic Agentic Web prototype with event-driven state persistence and closed-loop decision, feedback, and resource tracing to validate LLM-orchestrated workflow automation under operational constraints.

Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology | Core Technical Lead | 2020-2022

Distributed Marine Radar Networking Key Technology Research

Led the deep learning-based ship trajectory data mining submodule, covering trajectory cleaning, behavioral feature engineering, pattern recognition, and anomaly detection as the core of the undergraduate thesis. Also developed a marine data acquisition prototype and trajectory-analysis algorithms with related patent outputs.

Zhanjiang Municipal Science and Technology Bureau | Core Technical Lead | 2021-2023

Multi-Source Heterogeneous Ocean Sensing Data Fusion Key Technology Research

Designed a blockchain-based ship data management system for tamper-proof provenance tracking and full-chain auditability of multi-source maritime sensing data. Developed a biomimetic robotic fish system as a mobile underwater sensing platform and investigated structured integration mechanisms for heterogeneous sensor streams.
