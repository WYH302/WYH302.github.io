Selected projects

Projects I have worked on

These projects put models to work on road and bridge monitoring, ship trajectories, stone-cutting layouts, and multi-agent simulation. Here is what I contributed to each.

Guangzhou Municipal Transportation Bureau commissioned project | Core technical contributor | 2024-2025

Road and bridge safety monitoring

I worked on road-hazard detection and ground-penetrating radar (GPR) defect analysis, including sample curation, detector training, crack and pothole recognition, and object-imaging preprocessing.

I also integrated a visualization interface combining live video, radar signals, automated alarms, and model inference results. The project produced three related invention-patent outputs.

Self-Initiated Research and Engineering Project

StoneVL-Nest / CF-SlabNest: stone-cutting layouts

This self-initiated prototype translates Chinese cutting orders and images of stone defects into cutting layouts. I designed the multimodal parsing and nesting pipeline, framing the task as maximum-count irregular nesting.

An independent geometry checker tests the generated layout against its constraints and produces a verification certificate. I want to know whether the pieces can actually be cut as specified, not just whether the layout looks plausible.

Self-Initiated Research and Engineering Project

Multi-agent simulation and workflow prototypes

I built a multi-agent social simulation prototype with React, Phaser, and Node.js. Characters retain memories, schedule dialogue, and develop relationships through repeated interaction.

I also co-designed a deterministic Agentic Web prototype that persists state through event records and traces decisions, feedback, and resource use. These prototypes provide a controlled setting for studying agent interactions and failure points in LLM-orchestrated workflows.

Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology | Main R&D contributor | 2020-2022

Ship trajectories and marine radar

I contributed to ship-trajectory data mining, working on trajectory cleaning, behavioral features, pattern recognition, and anomaly detection. This work formed the core of my undergraduate thesis.

I also developed a marine data acquisition prototype, trajectory clustering, and clustering-then-discrimination prediction algorithms, with related invention-patent outputs in marine trajectory analysis.

Zhanjiang Municipal Science and Technology Bureau | Main R&D contributor | 2021-2023

Ocean sensing and robotic fish

I designed a blockchain-based ship data management system to track the origin and handling of multi-source maritime sensing data, supporting tamper-resistant provenance and auditing.

I also developed a biomimetic robotic fish as a mobile underwater sensing platform and investigated ways to combine heterogeneous sensor streams. This work includes a related mobile underwater sensing platform patent output.
