Submitted manuscript | First author | Image and Vision Computing

MC-SAM: Stable Adaptation of Segment Anything for Camouflaged Scene Segmentation

Studies parameter-efficient SAM adaptation for camouflaged object segmentation with a frozen 636M backbone and 5.1M trainable parameters. COD10K, NC4K, CAMO, and CHAMELEON experiments report COD10K differences versus MM-SAM of +0.8% S-measure, +5.0% E-measure, +6.2% weighted F, and -21.7% MAE.

current manuscript Image and Vision Computing segmentation

Submitted manuscript | First author | IEEE BIBM 2026

MECR: Evidence-Context Refinement for Few-Shot Fluorescence EEM Classification

Proposes MECR, a two-stage framework that aggregates multi-scale spectral evidence and fuses target-level chemical context to refine probability distributions. Public-16 experiments across four source families, 16 targets, and 80 folds report 0.9199 Macro-F1, 0.9397 accuracy, and 0.1763 NLL.

Submitted manuscript | First author | CIKM 2026

Auditable Neural Equation-DAG Export for Scientific Data Mining

Frames checkpoint-to-equation-DAG export as an audit problem using EML-KAN rather than post-hoc symbolic search. Evaluation covers 10 equation-native functions, 36 Feynman clean12 runs, SRSD splits, and export-length diagnostics alongside fidelity checks.

Submitted manuscript | First author | CIKM 2026

TyphoFormer++: Hurricane Track Forecasting under a Strict HURDAT2 Protocol

Defines a leakage-controlled HURDAT2 protocol with storm-level splits, train-only statistics, and observed-coordinate decoding. Local 2022-2024 Atlantic evaluation reports 34.15, 86.71, 156.20, and 230.53 km at 6, 12, 18, and 24 hours.

Submitted manuscript | First author | CIKM 2026

When Does Retrieval Help Missing-History Trajectory Prediction?

Studies retrieval for missing-history pedestrian prediction using train-only memory and history-only retrieval keys. ETH/UCY Easy/Hard experiments audit leakage controls and report condition-dependent effects, with Hard cases showing weak history-future alignment.

Submitted manuscript | Second author | ICAIF 2026

Noise Refined is News Aligned: ARGS for Financial Edge-Streaming Forecasting

Contributed to ARGS, a compact GHA-based white-box processor for financial edge-streaming forecasting. SPY and TSLA experiments are evaluated under the manuscript protocol through return, Sharpe ratio, and maximum-drawdown diagnostics.

Submitted manuscript | First author | ACL 2026

PressureTest-Biz: A Matched-Scenario Benchmark for Business Decision Stress Response in Large Language Models

Constructs PressureTest-Biz from 1,000 business cases and 4,000 matched pressure prompts. Across three target models and three judges, the analysis covers 12,000 responses and 36,000 records and reports a DeepSeek-chat BRisk shift from 0.0312 to 0.1490, while Gemini and GPT remain near floor.

Submitted manuscript | First author | CIKM 2026 Short Paper Track

De-Civ Lite: False-Acceptance-Aware Claim Anchoring

Studies pre-storage claim routing before durable AI or knowledge base storage, including accept, reject, revise, and defer actions. DeCivClaim-450 simulations report 0.7550 accuracy and 0.0831 false-acceptance rate with 10-human and four-LLM replay checks.
